- No source faces were rescaled or reshaped to fit existing plots.

## Historical-site registration screening
The existing road-derived source-to-atlas registration was used to examine source-instance candidate positions. A first-pass screening counted 38 positions apparently clear of earlier approximate road/water/footprint masks. **A secondary known-buildable-block check ruled out all 38 as automatically usable**: they fall outside the candidate road-excluded city blocks, some visibly offshore. Final provisional screening:
- 38 outside known buildable blocks
- 26 existing-building conflicts
- 27 shoreline review
- 14 road review

These statuses are only conservative QA filters, not official source locations or proof of invalidity. No automatic placement is authorised.

## QA and limits
Portable HTML passed Playwright desktop/mobile Canvas fallback checks for location tags, roads, model selection, rotation, draft-saving and zero JS page errors. Browser administrator restrictions prevented an independent HTTP-based modular test; live hardware WebGL2 remains unverified.

A draft is **never an approved placement**. The on-map source shape is a schematic, exact-source-size preview. Its collision warning is preliminary; actual source-face mesh collision, legal redistribution, building suitability, shoreline, terrain and confirmed V21 road geometry need review.

**Publication boundary:** this public document contains no original source meshes, large model geometry, footprint arrays, original SKP file, tile assets or working private viewer HTML.
