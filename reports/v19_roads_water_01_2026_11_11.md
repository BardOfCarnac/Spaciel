**11 November 2026 · status: integrated candidate, WebGL2 appearance NOT independently verified**

The normal approved **V19 map** remains the application shell. Native architecture from the previous V19 integration is preserved. Two new experimental layers have been integrated into its existing WebGL2 terrain and camera renderer, rather than embedding another map or the architecture workbench.

## Roads
- V17 is embedded as the offline fallback, with **2,422** centreline chains, **548 major** and **1,874 local**.
- V17 lines are mapped from the 5175×7966 atlas to V19 world coordinates using V19's existing registered bitmap-to-world transform; draped heights are sampled bilinearly from V19's 446×558 50-m terrain grid.
- Renderer uses constant physical-width GPU ribbons (provisional ~15 m major, ~9 m local), mitred continuous joins and optional close-range pavement-edge highlights. Visible road strength is adjustable. This is a surface rendering prototype, not verified real kerb geometry or grade engineering.
- V21 is an explicitly selected **online experimental branch source**; if retrieval fails, it does not silently replace V17. The branch stores its road data as `v20Roads` in the V21 cartographic grammar viewer, with 2,321 nonempty chains. Its atlas registration and road/building interactions remain experimental.
- V17 centreline validation against the V19 map underlay looks broadly coherent. **79** sampled V17 road vertices touch shoreline classes 1/2/3; do not assume these are all incorrect (bridges and coast approaches exist).
- Original painted atlas roads can still appear beneath the new 3D surfaces. The new overlay can be switched off when comparing.

## Water
- Shader material taken from the V3.4 coastline seam-fix prototype, including **1112×1392** source-grid coastal distance field aligned to V19's authoritative shoreline-class map.
- Retains irregular world-space noise, soft shelf/depth colour bands, mixed petrol-blue / muted cola phase, and separate animated glints. Initial **band strength 40%**, **shelf reach 36%**, reduced warm colour effect and adjustable sun angle.
- Uses registered water classes for wet/land selection: **does not warp or re-outline the shoreline**.
- If the new water shader is rejected by a GPU, the original V19 terrain shader is compiled as the fallback; if the new water distance texture is unavailable, the original V19 water colour remains.
- Actual physical bathymetry is not claimed. At close zoom the 1112×1392 source grid may still reveal coastline stair-stepping; await real-device visual review.

## Preserved features
V19 viewer's 661 pinned location markers, tag/category roles, 720 ms overlap morph, bottom spectrum bar, bubbles, ghost geography labels, unified search, existing coastline/terrain and 7,981 direct-SketchUp face placements across 60 tiles. 11 shoreline holds, protected Hot Zone mass and 100 unplaced large models remain unchanged. No existing road, building, or POI coordinates were moved.
